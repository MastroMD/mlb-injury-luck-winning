## Where the gap comes from

Almost all of the difference in level and spread sits in the episode source and end rule (steps 3–4). Placement counts barely move (26.4 → 25.8 episodes per club-season); episode length does (35.3 → 49.7 club games per episode).

Matched episodes (same player, club and season, starts within 7 days; 6931 of 7693 list stints matched): the feed episode is longer by a median of 3 games and a mean of 10.7; 27% are longer by 10 or more games, 0.5% shorter by 10 or more, and 45% agree within 2 games. The public end rule therefore runs long relative to the list: an episode stays open until an activation or a first game back is seen. Likely reasons the feed misses a return (not verified here): activations recorded in other wording, or a player optioned or outrighted off the IL without an activation line.

## Wins per WAR lost: attenuation or scale?

On the same 270 club-seasons and F1's controls, the coefficient is -0.757 with v1's WAR lost and -0.553 with F1's (the v1 value differs slightly from the published v1 coefficient because the controls here are F1's roster projection, whose exposure term comes from the feed episodes). The two measures correlate 0.913 (within-season rank correlation 0.930; 0.899 after removing the controls).

- **Scale.** Conditional on the controls, one v1 WAR lost corresponds to 1.10 F1 WAR lost. Rescaling alone moves -0.757 to -0.687 (this is also the IV estimate, F1 instrumented by v1).
- **Noise.** Multiplying by the conditional reliability (r² = 0.808) gives -0.555, against the observed -0.553. A scale difference plus classical measurement error is consistent with the whole gap (this assumes the two builds' errors are independent, which they are not entirely: both use the same projections).
- **Preregistered disattenuated value.** β / λ with λ = cov(F1ᵣ, v1ᵣ) / var(F1ᵣ) = 0.733 gives -0.754. That λ mixes scale and noise, so this value is in v1 units; the F1-unit correction is the IV value -0.687. Both use the third-party list and are sensitivities, never the headline.
- Split-half reliability: not used: a split of a club-season's episodes measures which players were hurt, which is real WAR lost, not measurement error, so it cannot estimate the reliability that matters for attenuation.

## Reading

The public build finds more WAR lost per club-season than v1 because its episodes are longer, not because it finds more placements. The same longer, noisier durations shrink the wins coefficient. The two effects roughly offset in the product: one SD of injury luck in wins is 2.07 on F1 and 2.31 on v1 (SD × |wins per WAR| at each end of the waterfall). Ranks agree closely, so club-level conclusions travel between builds; levels do not, and the paper quotes F1 levels only.
